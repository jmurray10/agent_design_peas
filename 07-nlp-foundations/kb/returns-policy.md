# Returns and refunds

Any item bought from the online store can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full
refund, provided it is in resalable condition with the original packaging.

Laptops, tablets and other opened electronics have a shorter 14 day return window. The
window starts on the delivery date, not the order date.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method and take 5 to 7 business days to appear on
a statement after the returned item reaches our warehouse.

Items marked final sale on the product page cannot be returned or exchanged. Gift cards are
also non-returnable.
