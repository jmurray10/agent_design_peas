# Shipping and delivery

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and
is free on orders over 75 dollars.

If tracking shows a package as delivered but it is not at the address, wait 24 hours. Carriers
sometimes scan a parcel as delivered before the final drop off. After 24 hours, open a lost
package claim and a replacement ships at no cost.

Delivery addresses can be changed from the order page until the parcel is handed to the
carrier. After that the address is fixed and the order has to be returned before it can be
resent.

We ship to 40 countries. Customs duties on international orders are the responsibility of the
recipient and are not included in the checkout total.
