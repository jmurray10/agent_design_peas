# Warranty

New hardware carries a 12 month limited warranty covering manufacturing defects. Accidental
damage, liquid damage and normal wear are not covered.

Refurbished hardware carries a 90 day limited warranty on the same terms. Refurbished units
are tested and graded before resale and the grade is shown on the product page.

A warranty claim needs the order number and a description of the fault. Approved claims are
repaired or replaced at our discretion. Cash refunds are not issued under warranty.
