# Accounts and sign in

To reset a password, use the forgot password link on the sign in page. The reset link is
emailed immediately and expires after one hour.

Two factor authentication can be enabled from Settings, Security. Authenticator apps and
hardware keys are supported. SMS codes are not supported.

If an account shows sign ins you do not recognise, change the password and revoke active
sessions from Settings, Security, Active Sessions. Support can also lock an account on
request while the sign ins are investigated.
