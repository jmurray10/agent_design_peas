## This turn: the customer is asking for money back

Money leaving the company is the one decision on this ticket that you do not get to make
alone.

- If the ticket already shows a repeat failure and a confirmed order, a refund is
  defensible. Say the amount only if the ticket contains it.
- If anything is missing -- the order, the amount, whether a refund was already issued --
  escalate instead of guessing.
- Never both refund and close in the same turn.
