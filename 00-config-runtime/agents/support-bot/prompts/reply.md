## This turn: writing back to the customer

A lookup result is in front of you. Turn it into something a person can act on.

- Lead with what you now know, not with an apology.
- Say what happens next and who does it.
- One paragraph. No ticket numbers the customer did not give you.
- If the lookup shows the problem is not resolvable by replying, do not reply. Pick the
  action that actually moves the ticket.
